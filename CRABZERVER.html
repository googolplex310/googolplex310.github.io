Background

This collaborative senior design project was developed to address a practical problem faced by recreational crab fishermen. During the project's planning phase, our team explored several ideas before identifying an opportunity to create a solution that could provide meaningful value to a specific community. Through discussions with a recreational fisherman and research into traditional crabbing practices, we learned that checking crab pots is often repetitive, physically demanding, and inefficient, requiring fishermen to retrieve pots at arbitrary intervals without knowing whether they contain crabs. These insights inspired the development of the CrabZerver—a smart monitoring system designed to improve the crabbing experience. By combining underwater imaging with automated monitoring and user notifications, the system allows fishermen to observe activity around their crab pots and receive alerts when it is likely that crabs have been captured. Rather than repeatedly checking empty pots, users can make more informed decisions about when to retrieve them, reducing unnecessary effort while providing greater visibility into underwater conditions.
