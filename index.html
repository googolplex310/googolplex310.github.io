My Story

My name is Jonathan Lopez, and I am recently graduated from CSULB. I double majored in Computer Engineering (B.S.) and Physics (B.A.) and graduated in Spring of 2026 with honors. I have had a deep interest in STEM since my youth, but I am particularly interested in a career as an embedded systems or electronic warfare engineer.

I grew up in Compton, California, attended locals schools from pre-k to 12th grade, then continued my education locally at El Camino College. Then, I transfered to CSULB for my undergrad where I recently graduated from in Spring of 2026. In my youth, I have always had an affinity for science, have been described as generally inquisitive, and embrace challenges, which are qualities that I am still deeply passionate of. On my downtime I enjoy tinkering with microcontrollers and microprocessors, like the Tiva TM4C123G or Raspberry Pi 5. Some projects that exemplify this can be found here.

Outside of technical areas I enjoy scuba diving, playing ice hockey and lacrosse, and hiking. I mostly dive near Palos Verdes, close to Redondo Beach in Southern California, due to its accessibility. This activity has given me an appreciation for marine conservation and the marvels curated by nature in these ecological spaces. Under this vein, I wish to preserve these environments while exploring hardware design challenges and improve our understanding of nature and provide an untapped view of marine landscapes.
